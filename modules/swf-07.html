Module 07 · 4 hours · Learn → Practice → Apply

Health & Wellness Support

The physical and mental health barriers that can keep women from working, and how to help them get care and build routines that support a job. You'll build a wellness resource sheet as a team for a new mother starting work.

How the four hours flow

Open · 20 minWelcome and opening circle
Learn · 70 minWomen's health and work, health effects of violence, getting care, and wellness routines
Practice · 90 minTeam roles: Patrice's wellness plan

Includes 15 minutes of breaks. There is no homework: everything is submitted by the end of class. Practice cases are fictional. Never bring real client information to class.

By the end of this moduleLearning objectives

The learner will be able to:

  1. Identify physical and mental health barriers to employment, including women's health, pregnancy and postpartum needs, and the health effects of violence.
  2. Support participants in accessing health care and benefits.
  3. Encourage wellness routines that support job retention.

Practice · in class · groups of 4–6Team roles: Patrice's wellness plan

Patrice, 26 (fictional), had a baby four months ago and starts a job as a pharmacy technician trainee in two weeks. She left an abusive relationship while pregnant.

Each person on your team plays someone supporting Patrice's health. Your role card has facts only you know. Together, your team builds a wellness resource sheet and plan that helps Patrice start, and keep, her new job.

  1. In role, on your own (15 min): read your card and decide what health need your role sees first.
  2. Team huddle (35 min): share what you know and fill in the wellness resource sheet together.
  3. Plan and present (25 min): write Patrice's plan for her first two weeks and how her routines fit.
  4. Debrief (15 min): step out of role and talk about what it was like on the other side of the desk.
