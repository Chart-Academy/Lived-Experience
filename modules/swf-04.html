Communication & Group Facilitation

The listening skills that build trust one-on-one, and the facilitation skills that make a women's group feel safe. You'll co-facilitate a segment of a workforce readiness group, handle common challenges, and give each other feedback.

By the end of this moduleLearning objectives

The learner will be able to:

  1. Use active listening, open questions, and plain language.
  2. Set group agreements and manage participation in a women's workforce readiness group.
  3. Respond to disclosure in a group respectfully and safely.
  4. Adapt communication for culture, literacy, and disability.

Practice · in class · groups of 4–6Team roles: co-facilitate the Pathways Circle

The Pathways Circle (fictional) is a weekly workforce readiness group for women survivors. Today's 15-minute segment: “Talking about gaps in your work history.”

Two people co-facilitate while the others play participants with challenges on their cards. After each round, the observer gives feedback and the roles rotate, so at least four people get to lead.

Observer and timekeeper

  1. Prepare (20 min): co-facilitators plan their opening and question. Participants read their cards.
  2. Three rounds (50 min): run the 15-minute segment, get feedback, and rotate co-facilitators.
  3. Plan and present (40 min): share what worked for each challenge and your best one-sentence answer about a work gap.
  4. Debrief (30 min): step out of role and talk about what it was like to lead, and to be led.
