HS-BCP Exam Readiness

What the HS-BCP exam asks of you, where you're strong, and where to study. You'll learn test strategies for scenario questions, coach each other through practice questions as a study squad, and leave with a credential plan with real dates.

Practice · in class · groups of 4–6Study squad: coach the team

Your team becomes a study squad. Each person is the coach for one part of the course, with practice questions written for this course (not real exam questions).

Each coach reads their questions to the team, everyone answers on their own, then the coach walks the team through the best answer using the scenario strategy. Every learner tracks their own results to find their two weakest areas.

Strategy and time

  1. Prepare to coach (20 min): read your coach card, practice reading the questions aloud, and plan how you'll explain the best answer.
  2. Study squad rounds (40 min): each coach leads their questions. Everyone answers on their own, then the coach explains. Track your results.
  3. Write and trade (30 min): your team writes one new practice question and trades it with another team, then shares top strategies.
  4. Debrief (25 min): talk about how it felt to coach, and what your results told you.
