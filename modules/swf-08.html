Module 08 · 6 hours · Learn → Practice → Apply

Documentation & Resource Referral

How to write objective, strengths-based progress notes that protect a woman's privacy and safety, and how to make referrals that connect. You'll fix a set of weak notes as a team, write referrals, and track them.

By the end of this moduleLearning objectives

The learner will be able to:

  1. Write objective, strengths-based progress notes.
  2. Make referrals to DC employment, reentry, housing, and women's and survivor services, with a warm handoff.
  3. Track referrals and follow-ups in a shared log.
  4. Proofread and revise their own writing for clarity and a professional tone.

Practice · in class · groups of 4–6Team roles: fix the notes and referrals for Yvonne

Yvonne, 42 (fictional), is a survivor of labor trafficking who has been in a women's workforce program for two months. Several staff wrote notes and referrals for her this week, and every one has problems.

Each person on your team plays one of those staff members. Your card has your weak note or referral and what really happened. Together, your team fixes them, writes one strong progress note, and builds a referral log.

Signs off and builds the log

  1. In role, on your own (20 min): read your weak note or referral and what really happened. List every problem.
  2. Team huddle (50 min): run the four checks on every note and rewrite each one together.
  3. Plan and present (40 min): write one strong progress note, a safe referral email, and a referral log, then present.
  4. Debrief (30 min): step out of role and talk about what it's like to read what others write about you.

How it's graded

Your note · 5Three real problems matched to the right check, and a rewrite that passes all four.
Progress note · 5Facts, progress with strengths, and next steps with who and when.
Referral and log · 5A professional email that shares only what Yvonne agreed to, and a complete log.
Reflection · 5Thoughtful, specific, and in the learner's own words.
