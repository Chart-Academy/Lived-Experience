Module 05 · 7 hours · Learn → Practice → Apply

Crisis Prevention & Behavior Support

How to notice early warning signs, calm a situation before it becomes a crisis, and support a woman afterward. You'll learn positive behavior support and practice de-escalation in three scenarios at a job training site.

Includes 60 minutes of breaks. There is no homework: everything is submitted by the end of class. Practice cases are fictional. Never bring real client information to class.

By the end of this moduleLearning objectives

The learner will be able to:

  1. Recognize early warning signs of escalation.
  2. Use verbal de-escalation techniques in role-play.
  3. Explain positive behavior support and why it replaces punishment.
  4. Know when and how to escalate to a supervisor, crisis line, or emergency services.

Practice · in class · groups of 4–6Team roles: three scenarios at Bright Start Training

Brianna, 24 (fictional), is in week three of a culinary job training program for women. She's talented, and she has had a hard week. Today, three things happen.

Your team acts out three scenarios. Each time, a different person plays the facilitator who de-escalates. Brianna's card tells her what she's feeling and what calms her. After each scenario, the team completes a debrief form.

  1. Read your cards (20 min): read your card and plan how you'll play it. Agree on the ground rules.
  2. Three scenarios (60 min): act out each scenario for 5 minutes, then complete a debrief form. Rotate the facilitator each time.
  3. Plan and present (45 min): share your team's best de-escalation phrases and a prevention plan for Brianna.
  4. Debrief (35 min): step out of role and talk about how it felt to be in the middle of a crisis.
