Safety & Safety Planning

How to help a woman build a safety plan that covers home, work, her commute, her children, and technology, and how to keep her location private in every record. You'll build a full safety plan as a team for a woman starting a new job.

By the end of this moduleLearning objectives

The learner will be able to:

  1. Help a woman build a safety plan that covers work, the commute, child care pickup, and technology, including stalking and location tracking.
  2. Identify workplace safety risks and emergency procedures.
  3. Protect participant location and contact information in all records.
  4. Connect participants to DC domestic violence and safety resources.

Practice · in class · groups of 4–6Team roles: Lena's safety plan

Lena, 35 (fictional), starts a new job at a hospital laundry next Monday. She left her abusive husband five months ago. He still lives in the same neighborhood, and they share custody of their 6-year-old son.

Each person on your team plays someone helping Lena get ready for her first day. Your role card has facts only you know. Together, your team builds a safety plan with Lena that covers home, the commute, work, child care pickup, and technology.

Writes the safety plan

  1. In role, on your own (20 min): read your card. What risk does your role see? What does Lena already do to stay safe?
  2. Team huddle (40 min): share what you know and build the safety plan setting by setting.
  3. Plan and present (30 min): check the plan against Lena's choices and present her first-day plan.
  4. Debrief (25 min): step out of role and talk about what it was like on the other side of the desk.
