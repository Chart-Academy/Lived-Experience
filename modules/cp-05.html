Case Management & Service Coordination

The full case management cycle, from intake to closing. You'll learn strengths-based assessment and SMART goals, then work as a team to build a 30-day service plan and a follow-up tracker for a family juggling several agencies at once.

By the end of this module

Learning objectives

The learner will be able to:

  1. Describe the stages of the case management cycle: intake, assessment, planning, linkage, monitoring, and closing.
  2. Complete a strengths-based assessment.
  3. Write a service plan with SMART goals.
  4. Coordinate services across two or more agencies and track follow-ups.

Practice · in class · groups of 4–6

Team roles: the Carter family's 30-day plan

Monique Carter, 58 (fictional), is raising her two grandchildren, ages 7 and 11, while their mother is in treatment. Monique works part time as a home health aide, is two months behind on rent, and has diabetes. Her 11-year-old has an IEP at school.

Each person on your team plays a practitioner from a different agency. Your role card has facts only you know. Together, your team builds Monique's 30-day service plan with SMART goals and a follow-up tracker.

Lead case manager (Team lead)

Runs the meeting and the tracker

  1. In role, on your own (25 min): read your card and write one SMART goal your agency could work on with Monique.
  2. Team huddle (75 min): assess strengths, find the conflicts between agencies, and agree on three to five SMART goals.
  3. Plan, track, and present (55 min): build the 30-day plan and enter every step in the follow-up tracker, then present.
  4. Debrief (35 min): step out of role and talk about what it's like to be the family in the middle.

Apply · 20 points · completed and submitted in class

Module 5 assignment

Capture the plan your team built for the Carter family, then connect case management to your own strengths. You'll complete it in the last part of class and upload it before you leave. There is no homework.

Part 1

Your agency's part

What your agency knew, your SMART goal, and how it depended on the others.

Part 2

Your team's 30-day plan

Strengths, three SMART goals, and the follow-up schedule your team built.

Part 3

Strengths in my own life

How strengths-based planning connects to your own life, in your own words.
