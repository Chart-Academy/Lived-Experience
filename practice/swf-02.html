Certified Survivor Workforce Facilitator · Module 02 · In-class packet

Team roles: Marisol's stabilization plan

For instructors: Print one packet per team. Cut the role cards apart and give one to each person. Any learner can switch to the Team lead role. Post 988, the National Domestic Violence Hotline, and the DC Victim Hotline in the room (confirm numbers before class).
Remember: Marisol is fictional. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case Meet Marisol

Marisol, 28, left an abusive partner six weeks ago with her children, Sofia (8) and Leo (3). They are staying in a domestic violence shelter with a 90-day limit. Her partner controlled all the money, and she has no bank account of her own. She worked as a home health aide for four years before he made her quit. She came to a women's workforce program and wants a job right away.

“I need my own money. I'm never depending on a man again. Just help me find a job.”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 20 min on your own

Shelter advocate

Domestic violence shelter

Only you know:

  • Marisol has 44 days left at the shelter.
  • Her ex has been driving past her old job and her sister's house.
  • The shelter's location is confidential. Marisol can't give it on job applications.

You need from another role: A plan for the address question and her commute.

Child and family worker

Family support program

Only you know:

  • Leo, 3, has no child care. There's a wait list for subsidized care, but survivors may get priority. Eligibility must be checked.
  • Sofia, 8, changed schools and is having nightmares.
  • Marisol asked about custody. Her ex has not filed anything yet.

You need from another role: Child care before any job can start, and a legal referral for custody questions.

Benefits outreach worker

Public benefits

Only you know:

  • Marisol has no income. She may qualify for SNAP, TANF, and Medicaid for herself and the children.
  • Her ID and the children's birth certificates are still at her ex's apartment.
  • She doesn't have a bank account. Her ex has wrecked her credit.

You need from another role: Replacement documents, and a safe way to get her things.

Community health worker

Women's health center

Only you know:

  • Marisol has headaches since her ex hit her head against a wall two months ago. She hasn't seen a doctor.
  • She says she sleeps 3 to 4 hours a night.
  • She told you, “I'm fine. I don't have time to be sick.”

You need from another role: A medical visit and support for sleep and stress, without pushing her.

Know your lane: a head injury needs a medical evaluation. Refer; don't diagnose.

Workforce facilitator

Women's workforce program

Only you know:

  • Marisol has four years of experience as a home health aide and liked the work.
  • Her certification lapsed. A refresher course takes two weeks.
  • A home care agency is hiring for day shifts, with flexible hours.

You need from another role: To know when she's ready, and what has to be in place first.

Team lead

Runs the planning meeting

Your job:

  • Keep time and make sure every role speaks.
  • Before ranking anything, ask: “What does Marisol say matters most?”
  • Write the team's ranking on the worksheet.

Only you know: Marisol told the shelter staff, “If I can't show my kids I can take care of us, what was the point of leaving?”
