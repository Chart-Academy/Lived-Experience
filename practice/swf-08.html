Certified Survivor Workforce Facilitator · Module 08 · In-class packet

Team roles: fix the notes and referrals for Yvonne

For instructors: Print one packet per team. Cut the role cards apart and give one to each person. The Team lead builds the referral log and the final progress note.
Remember: Yvonne is fictional. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case Meet Yvonne

Yvonne, 42, is a survivor of labor trafficking. For three years she was forced to work as a live-in housekeeper without pay. She now lives in confidential housing for survivors and has been in a women's workforce program for two months. She wants to work as a home health aide. This week, five staff members wrote notes or referrals about her. Every one of them has problems.

“The people who held me wrote everything down about me. I need to know what you're writing, and who sees it.”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 20 min on your own

Workforce facilitator

Progress note

Your note says:

  • “Yvonne is very paranoid about her info. Seemed checked out. Probably needs therapy. Talked about jobs.”

What really happened: Yvonne asked who can see her file (a reasonable question). She said she was tired from a night class. You reviewed three home health aide job postings, and she chose two to apply to by Friday 10/18.

Housing advocate

Progress note

Your note says:

  • “Visited Y at the safe house on Monroe St, apt 2B. Place is fine. She's doing ok.”

What really happened: You visited on 10/14 for 30 minutes. Her lease renewal is due 11/1. She asked for help understanding it. You'll review it together on 10/22.

Legal advocate

Referral email

Your email to a training program says:

  • Subject: “Yvonne Barnes, trafficking victim.” Body: “Please enroll her. She was trafficked for 3 years and has a T visa application pending. Very sweet lady.”

What really happened: Yvonne signed a release to share only her name, contact through the program, and that she is interested in the home health aide course. She did not agree to share anything about trafficking or immigration.

Health navigator

Referral log entry

Your log says:

  • “Ref'd to clinic. Will f/u.”

What really happened: On 10/15 you referred Yvonne to a community health center for a physical required by the training program. She signed consent. Her appointment is 10/24 at 9 a.m. You'll call her on 10/25 to check how it went.

Peer mentor

Progress note

Your note says:

  • “Y and I had a good talk. She told me everything about what happened to her and her family back home. So sad. She's a fighter!!”

What really happened: You met for 40 minutes on 10/16. Yvonne said she felt “less alone.” She wants to join the women's support group on Thursdays. Her story is hers. It doesn't belong in a progress note.

Team lead

Signs off and builds the log

Your job:

  • Keep time and make sure every role speaks.
  • Run the four checks on every note: accurate, objective, safe and private, professional.
  • Build the referral log and write the team's best progress note.

Only you know: Yvonne has asked to read her file. Program policy allows participants to see their own records.
