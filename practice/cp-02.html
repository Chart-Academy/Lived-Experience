Certified Community Practitioner · Module 02 · In-class packet

Team roles: Tanya's ethics case conference

For instructors: Print one packet per team. Cut the role cards apart and give one to each person. The Team lead holds the decision table. Confirm the CFSA hotline number before class.
Remember: Tanya is fictional. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case Meet Tanya

Tanya, 29, came to a neighborhood resource center for help with two months of back rent. She lives three blocks away with her 6-year-old son. She signed a release of information that lets the center share her information with the rental assistance program only. One of the center's outreach workers grew up in the neighborhood and knows Tanya's family from church. This week, several staff members each ran into an ethical question about Tanya's case. Today is the team's ethics case conference.

“I almost didn't come in. Everybody around here knows everybody. I need to know my business stays my business.”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 20 min on your own

Intake coordinator

Front desk and intake

Only you know:

  • Tanya's cousin called yesterday and asked, “Is Tanya getting help there? Her mom wants to know.”
  • You said, “I think so, let me check,” then stopped yourself and said you'd call back.
  • Tanya's release covers the rental assistance program only.

You need from another role: Advice on what to say when you call the cousin back, and whether to tell Tanya.

Outreach worker

Grew up in the neighborhood

Only you know:

  • You've known Tanya's family from church for 15 years. Tanya's aunt is your mother's friend.
  • Tanya sent you a friend request on social media last night.
  • She offered to braid your daughter's hair for free “to say thank you.”
  • You haven't told your supervisor you know the family.

You need from another role: A plan for the dual relationship that keeps Tanya's trust.

Housing specialist

Rental assistance

Only you know:

  • Tanya's landlord called and asked you to send “her full history” before he agrees to a payment plan.
  • Tanya asked you to leave a past eviction off her rental assistance application because “it was years ago.”
  • The application asks about past evictions and says answers must be true.

You need from another role: What you can share with the landlord, and how to talk with Tanya about the eviction question.

Family support worker

Home visits

Only you know:

  • On a home visit, Tanya's son had a bruise on his upper arm.
  • When Tanya went to answer the door, he said, “Mom's boyfriend gets real mad sometimes.”
  • Tanya seemed nervous and asked you not to mention the boyfriend to anyone.

You need from another role: The team to confirm your next steps today, and how to stay honest with Tanya.

This is a mandated reporting situation. The team decides how, not whether.

Program assistant

Events and records

Only you know:

  • A coworker posted photos from the center's back-to-school event. Tanya and her son are in one, with the center's name in the caption.
  • Tanya didn't sign a photo release.
  • In the break room, two staff talked about Tanya's rent situation by name while a volunteer was there.

You need from another role: What to do about the photo, and how to raise the break-room talk.

Team lead

Runs the case conference

Your job:

  • Keep time and make sure every role speaks.
  • Walk the team through the six steps for each question.
  • Write each decision on the team's decision table.

Only you know: agency policy says staff may accept thank-you cards but not services or gifts worth more than $10, and dual relationships must be reported to a supervisor.
