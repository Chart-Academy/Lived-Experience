Certified Community Practitioner · Module 08 · In-class packet

Study squad: coach the team

For instructors: Print one packet per team. Cut the coach cards apart and give one to each person. The practice questions were written for this course and are not real exam questions. Before class, check CCE's current HS-BCP information and update the slides if anything has changed.
Remember: All practice questions and people in them are written for this course. They are not real exam questions. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case Your study squad

Your team is a study squad. Each person is the coach for one part of the course. The coach reads each practice question aloud. Everyone writes their answer on their own, without talking. Then the coach reveals the best answer and explains why, using the scenario strategy. Each learner tracks their own results on the team worksheet. These questions were written for this course. They are not real exam questions.

“I know this stuff from living it. I just need to learn how the test wants me to say it.”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 20 min on your own

Ethics and boundaries coach

Modules 1 and 2

Your questions (read aloud, then reveal):

  • Q1. A client's sister calls and asks whether her brother kept his appointment. What should you do? A) Tell her yes, since she's family. B) Say you can't confirm whether someone receives services. C) Ask the client later if it was okay. D) Transfer her to your supervisor. Best: B. Even confirming someone is a client breaks confidentiality without a release.
  • Q2. A client offers you $50 to thank you for your help. What is the best response? A) Accept it so you don't offend her. B) Accept and give it to the agency. C) Thank her warmly and explain you can't accept money, following agency policy. D) Ignore the offer. Best: C. Decline respectfully and follow policy.

Trauma-informed practice coach

Module 3

Your questions (read aloud, then reveal):

  • Q1. A client goes silent and stares at the floor when you ask about her housing history. What should you do first? A) Repeat the question more slowly. B) Move to the next section. C) Pause, check in, and offer her a choice to take a break or skip it. D) Note that she is uncooperative. Best: C. This may be a freeze response. Offer safety and choice.
  • Q2. Which practice best supports “trustworthiness and transparency”? A) Explaining what will happen in the meeting and how long it will take. B) Keeping the intake form private from the client. C) Asking all questions in one visit. D) Using clinical terms. Best: A.

Communication coach

Module 4

Your questions (read aloud, then reveal):

  • Q1. Which is an open question? A) “Do you have a job?” B) “Are you okay?” C) “What has your work been like this year?” D) “Did you go to the clinic?” Best: C.
  • Q2. A client who speaks limited English brings her 12-year-old to interpret. What should you do? A) Use the child, since she's there. B) Arrange a trained interpreter or language line. C) Speak slowly and loudly in English. D) Reschedule without explaining. Best: B. Don't use children as interpreters.

Case management coach

Module 5

Your questions (read aloud, then reveal):

  • Q1. Which goal is SMART? A) “Client will find housing.” B) “Client will feel better.” C) “Client will submit the rental assistance application, with the case manager, by May 15.” D) “Client will try to work.” Best: C.
  • Q2. You referred a client to a job program two weeks ago. What is the next step in the case management cycle? A) Close the case. B) Follow up to see if she connected and how it's going. C) Make a new referral. D) Wait for the program to call you. Best: B. Monitoring.

Resources and documentation coach

Modules 6 and 7

Your questions (read aloud, then reveal):

  • Q1. Which is the best example of a warm handoff? A) Giving the client a phone number. B) Emailing the client a list. C) With consent, calling the agency together and introducing the client. D) Telling the client to walk in. Best: C.
  • Q2. Which case note sentence is written best? A) “Client was hostile.” B) “Client raised his voice and said, ‘Nobody helps me.’” C) “Client had an attitude.” D) “Client seemed upset, probably drinking.” Best: B. Facts, not labels.

Team lead and test coach

Strategy and time

Your job:

  • Keep time: about 6 minutes per coach.
  • Before each reveal, ask: “What's the last line asking? What are the key facts? What can we cross out?”
  • Make sure everyone writes their own answer before the reveal.

Only you know: after all the coaches finish, the team writes one practice question of its own for another team to answer.
