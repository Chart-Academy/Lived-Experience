Certified Survivor Workforce Facilitator · Module 07 · In-class packet

Team roles: Patrice's wellness plan

For instructors: Print one packet per team. Cut the role cards apart. Remind learners to support and refer, not diagnose. Confirm DC health resources before class.
Remember: Patrice is fictional. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case Meet Patrice

Patrice, 26, had her son, Malik, four months ago. She left an abusive relationship while pregnant and lives with her grandmother. She starts a job as a pharmacy technician trainee in two weeks, on day shifts. She is breastfeeding and hasn't had a postpartum checkup. She told her facilitator she's excited about the job but some days can barely get out of bed.

“I want this job so bad. But some days I can barely get out of bed, and I don't know if that's normal.”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 15 min on your own

Community health worker

Women's health center

Only you know:

  • Patrice missed her postpartum checkup because she had no ride.
  • Her Medicaid coverage for pregnancy may continue after birth. Check her current coverage.
  • The health center offers postpartum visits with a depression screening.

You need from another role: A ride, and an appointment before she starts work.

WIC and benefits worker

WIC program

Only you know:

  • Patrice and Malik are enrolled in WIC.
  • WIC offers breastfeeding support and can help with a breast pump.
  • Her WIC appointment is on a weekday morning, which will conflict with her new shift.

You need from another role: A new appointment time that fits her work schedule.

Peer mentor

Has lived experience as a young mother

Only you know:

  • Patrice told you she cries most days and feels like she's “failing Malik.”
  • She said she sometimes feels numb, and that she hasn't told anyone because she's afraid they'll take the baby.
  • You had postpartum depression and got help. You're comfortable sharing that briefly.

You need from another role: A gentle way to encourage her to talk to a doctor, without scaring her.

She has not said anything about hurting herself or the baby. If she does, follow the safety steps: stay with her, call 988, get your supervisor.

Workforce facilitator

Women's workforce program

Only you know:

  • The pharmacy is a large chain with an HR department.
  • Patrice is afraid to ask for pumping breaks in her first week.
  • Her training includes standing for long shifts and some evening study.

You need from another role: A plan for what Patrice can ask for at work, and how.

Child care navigator

Child care subsidy program

Only you know:

  • Malik has a spot at an infant care center starting next week, with a subsidy.
  • The center is a 20-minute bus ride from the pharmacy.
  • Patrice's grandmother can do pickup three days a week, not all five.

You need from another role: A backup plan for the other two days.

Team lead

Writes the wellness plan

Your job:

  • Keep time and make sure every role speaks.
  • Build the wellness resource sheet: need, resource, how to connect, workplace support.
  • Keep asking: “Is this realistic for a new mother starting a new job?”

Only you know: Patrice said the thing that helps her most is her Sunday walk with her grandmother and Malik.
