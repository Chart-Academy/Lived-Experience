Certified Survivor Workforce Facilitator · Module 01 · In-class packet

Team roles: ethics at Women Rising

For instructors: Print one packet per team. Cut the role cards apart and give one to each person. Any learner can swap to the Team lead role if a card feels too close to home. Bring printed copies of the current NADSP Code of Ethics.
Remember: Angela is fictional. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case Meet Angela

Angela, 31, joined Women Rising, a workforce program for women survivors, after leaving an abusive relationship two years ago and spending 18 months in a shelter. She now lives in transitional housing. She worked in restaurants for 10 years and wants a full-time job with health insurance. This month, several staff members each ran into an ethical question about her case. Today is the team meeting.

“For years someone else made every decision for me. I need people who will let me make my own, even the wrong ones.”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 20 min on your own

Workforce facilitator

Angela's main contact

Only you know:

  • Angela invited you to her church's women's day and to her birthday dinner.
  • She texts you on your personal phone at night. You gave her the number during a hard week.
  • You like her a lot and don't want to hurt her feelings.

You need from another role: A respectful way to reset the relationship.

Job developer

Works with employers

Only you know:

  • A hotel wants to interview Angela for a full-time job with benefits.
  • The hiring manager asked you, “Is she one of the domestic violence women? I need to know if there'll be drama.”
  • Angela has not signed a release to share anything about her history.

You need from another role: What to say to the hiring manager, and what to tell Angela.

Housing advocate

Transitional housing

Only you know:

  • A staff member wants to make counseling a requirement for Angela to keep her housing.
  • Angela said she isn't ready for counseling and gets support from her church group.
  • Program rules don't require counseling.

You need from another role: The team to decide whose choice this is.

Peer mentor

Has lived experience of leaving abuse

Only you know:

  • Angela's story is very close to yours. You've been thinking about her case at night.
  • You've been telling her what you did when you left, in a lot of detail.
  • Last week she said, “I'm not you. Stop telling me what to do.”

You need from another role: Help sorting out what is for Angela and what is for you.

Program assistant

Front desk and events

Only you know:

  • You heard two staff joking about “the drama ladies” in the break room.
  • Staff tell their favorite participants about job leads first.
  • Angela asked you why other women always seem to hear about jobs before she does.

You need from another role: How to raise these concerns, and with whom.

Team lead

Runs the meeting

Your job:

  • Keep time and make sure every role speaks.
  • For each question, ask: “Which tenet applies? What would Angela want?”
  • Write each decision on the team's ethics table.

Only you know: the hotel's application asks whether applicants have ever had a protection order. Women Rising has advocated with other employers to remove questions like this.
