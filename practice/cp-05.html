Certified Community Practitioner · Module 05 · In-class packet

Team roles: the Carter family's 30-day plan

For instructors: Print one packet per team. Cut the role cards apart and give one to each person. Each team needs one laptop to build the tracker in Google Sheets from the template on the module page.
Remember: The Carter family is fictional. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case Meet the Carter family

Monique Carter, 58, is raising her grandchildren, Jaylen (11) and Amari (7), while their mother is in a residential treatment program. Monique works part time as a home health aide, mostly weekday mornings. She is two months behind on rent, has diabetes, and has missed her last two doctor visits. Jaylen has an Individualized Education Program (IEP) and started a new school this fall. Several agencies are involved, and none of them are talking to each other. Your team meets to build one 30-day plan.

“I raised my kids. I can raise these two. I just can't do it with five offices calling me at work.”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 25 min on your own

Housing stability specialist

Emergency rental assistance program

Only you know:

  • Monique owes about $2,400 in back rent. The landlord sent a notice but has not filed in court yet.
  • Rental assistance needs: photo ID, the lease, the landlord's notice, proof of income, and proof of the grandchildren in the home.
  • Funding is first come, first served this month.

You need from another role: Proof that the grandchildren live with Monique.

Kinship care navigator

Kinship caregiver support program

Only you know:

  • Monique has no legal paperwork showing she is the children's caregiver.
  • She may qualify for kinship caregiver support, which can include a monthly payment. Eligibility must be checked.
  • The children's mother agrees with the arrangement and will sign papers at her treatment program.

You need from another role: A time when Monique can visit the treatment program, outside her work hours.

Benefits outreach worker

Public benefits service center

Only you know:

  • Monique gets SNAP for herself only. The grandchildren were never added.
  • Adding them could increase her benefits and give the children Medicaid if they don't already have coverage.
  • The renewal interview can be done by phone.

You need from another role: The kinship paperwork, and the children's birth certificates or Social Security numbers.

Community health worker

Neighborhood health center

Only you know:

  • Monique's blood sugar was high at her last visit, six months ago.
  • She missed two appointments because they were during her work hours.
  • The clinic has early evening hours on Wednesdays.
  • She told you, “If I get sick, who takes care of the kids?”

You need from another role: A plan that protects Monique's own health too.

School family liaison

Jaylen's school

Only you know:

  • Jaylen's IEP came from his old school. A new IEP meeting must be scheduled.
  • He has been late 6 times because Monique works early. The school has a before-care program.
  • Jaylen told the counselor he worries about his mom.

You need from another role: Monique's availability for the IEP meeting, and her consent to share with other agencies.

Lead case manager (Team lead)

Runs the meeting and the tracker

Your job:

  • Keep time and make sure every role speaks.
  • Write the SMART goals on the team worksheet.
  • Enter every action step in the team's Google Sheets tracker.

Only you know: Monique asked for one person to call her, only on her lunch break (12:00 to 12:30), and only by text first.
