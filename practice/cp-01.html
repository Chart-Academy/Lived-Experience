Backup Sample job postings · fictional, for practice

Community Resource Navigator · Southeast Family Center

Meet with families to identify needs and connect them to housing, food, and health resources. Help clients complete benefit applications. Make referrals and follow up within 7 days. Keep accurate case notes in our database. Build relationships with partner agencies. Lived experience with DC systems is valued. High school diploma required; associate degree preferred.

Case Management Aide · Northwest Reentry Partnership

Support case managers working with returning citizens. Help clients get ID documents and transportation. Schedule appointments and send reminders. Complete intake forms and update client files. Attend team meetings and report on client progress. Maintain confidentiality at all times. Experience with reentry services is a plus.

Outreach Specialist · Capitol Health Access Project

Reach residents in shelters and community sites to enroll them in health coverage. Explain eligibility in plain language. Help clients complete Medicaid applications. Track contacts and outcomes. Refer clients with urgent needs to clinical staff. Bilingual English and Spanish preferred. Must be comfortable working with people in crisis.
