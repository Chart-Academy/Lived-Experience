Certified Community Practitioner · Module 01 · In-class packet

Team Roles: Denise's Systems Map

For instructors: Print one packet per group. Cut the role cards apart along the dashed lines and give one to each person. Each card has facts only that role knows. The team map is shared, and the team lead fills it in.
Remember: Denise is fictional. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case Meet Denise

Denise, 34, is staying in a family shelter with her two children, ages 4 and 9. She came home from jail 10 months ago. She lost her retail job when the store closed, her SNAP benefits are about to run out, and her youngest child has asthma.

“Every office wants something from me before they'll help. I just need one person to help me figure out where to start.”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 15 min on your own

Housing navigator

Family shelter

Only you know:

  • Denise's shelter stay ends in 30 days.
  • She qualifies for a rapid rehousing program, but it needs proof of income or an active job search.
  • Staff have noticed her 4-year-old's asthma is worse since moving into the shelter.

You need from another agency: proof that Denise has income or is in a job program.

Benefits outreach worker

Public benefits service center

Only you know:

  • Her SNAP renewal interview is due in 10 days. She missed the last one because it was the same time as her supervision check-in.
  • She needs a valid photo ID to finish the interview.
  • She may qualify for cash assistance (TANF), which includes a work program.
  • Her children's Medicaid is active, but hers has lapsed.

You need from another agency: a valid photo ID.

Reentry navigator

Community reentry program

Only you know:

  • Denise checks in with her supervision officer every other Tuesday morning.
  • Her state ID expired while she was incarcerated, and her birth certificate is lost. Your program can pay the fees and help her request both.
  • She is nervous about how to answer questions about her record on job applications.

You need from another agency: appointment times that don't conflict with her Tuesday check-ins.

Community health worker

Neighborhood health center

Only you know:

  • Her 4-year-old has been seen twice in three months for asthma attacks. The doctor thinks dust or mold is a trigger.
  • Denise hasn't had a checkup since she came home.
  • She told you she isn't sleeping and feels overwhelmed.

You need from another agency: safer housing for the children and renewed coverage for Denise.

Know your lane: you don't diagnose. Who would you refer her to about sleep and stress?

Workforce specialist

American Job Center

Only you know:

  • Denise has 6 years of retail experience, including 2 years as an assistant shift lead.
  • A paid customer service training starts in 3 weeks, Monday to Thursday, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m.
  • She will need a photo ID for hiring paperwork and child care for her 4-year-old.

You need from another agency: an ID, child care, and a plan for her Tuesday check-ins.

Team lead

Runs the case meeting

Your job:

  • Keep time and make sure every role speaks.
  • Draw the team map, with Denise in the center.
  • Keep asking: “Who needs something that someone else at this table controls?”

Only you know: Denise told the shelter front desk she is tired of repeating her story to every office.
