Certified Survivor Workforce Facilitator · Module 06 · In-class packet

Team roles: Lena's safety plan

For instructors: Print one packet per team. Cut the role cards apart. Any learner can switch to the Team lead role. Confirm DC hotline numbers and programs before class and post them in the room.
Remember: Lena is fictional. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case Meet Lena

Lena, 35, left her abusive husband, Victor, five months ago. He still lives in their neighborhood, and they share custody of their son, Mateo (6), under a temporary court order. Lena starts a new job at a hospital laundry next Monday, on the 7 a.m. to 3:30 p.m. shift. She takes two buses to work and picks Mateo up from after-school care at 5 p.m. Your team is helping her get ready for her first day.

“I finally got a job. I'm not letting him take this from me too.”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 20 min on your own

Domestic violence advocate

Community DV program

Only you know:

  • Victor has violated the stay-away part of the court order once. Lena reported it.
  • Lena has a protection order that covers her home, but not her new workplace yet. It may be possible to add it.
  • Lena has a code word with her sister.

You need from another role: An updated order and a plan for custody exchanges.

Workforce facilitator

Women's workforce program

Only you know:

  • Lena hasn't told her new employer anything about Victor.
  • The hospital has security at the main entrance, but the laundry uses a back loading dock.
  • Lena is worried telling HR will make her look like “trouble.”

You need from another role: A plan for what, if anything, Lena tells her employer.

Hospital HR contact

Lena's new employer

Only you know:

  • The hospital has a workplace violence prevention policy and works with security on safety plans for employees.
  • Employees can request schedule changes for court dates.
  • HR keeps safety information confidential and shares it only with security, with the employee's consent.

You need from another role: Lena's consent before anything is shared.

After-school care director

Mateo's program

Only you know:

  • Victor is on Mateo's pickup list for his custody days.
  • Last week, Victor showed up on Lena's day and argued with staff.
  • The program needs a copy of the custody order to enforce pickup days.

You need from another role: A copy of the order and a plan for Lena's pickup days.

Tech safety specialist

Helps with phones and accounts

Only you know:

  • Lena's phone is still on Victor's family plan.
  • Her location sharing is on. Victor may be able to see where she is.
  • Mateo's tablet, which goes back and forth between homes, is logged into Victor's account.

You need from another role: A careful plan, made with the advocate, so Victor isn't alerted.

Team lead

Writes the safety plan

Your job:

  • Keep time and make sure every role speaks.
  • Go setting by setting: home, commute, work, child care, technology, and records.
  • Write the plan on the worksheet, and check every item with “Is this Lena's choice?”

Only you know: Lena's bus stop is two blocks from Victor's apartment.
