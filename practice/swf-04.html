Certified Survivor Workforce Facilitator · Module 04 · In-class packet

Team roles: co-facilitate the Pathways Circle

For instructors: Print one packet per team. Cut the cards apart. Rotate co-facilitators after each round so at least four learners lead. Watch the disclosure role-play closely and step in if needed.
Remember: The Pathways Circle and every participant in it are fictional. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case The Pathways Circle

The Pathways Circle is a weekly workforce readiness group for women survivors. Today's 15-minute segment is “Talking about gaps in your work history.” Two co-facilitators lead. The rest of the team plays participants. Each participant card has a challenge to bring to the group. After each round, the observer gives feedback, and new co-facilitators take over.

“Every interview, they ask about the gap. What am I supposed to say? That I was locked up? That he wouldn't let me work?”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 20 min on your own

Co-facilitator A

Opens and leads the activity

Your part:

  • Welcome, check-in round, and agreements reminder (4 minutes).
  • Introduce the topic and ask one open question to start.
  • Lead the pairs activity: practice a one-sentence answer about a work gap.

Co-facilitator B

Supports and closes

Your part:

  • Watch the group. Step in when someone needs support or the group goes off track.
  • Lead the share-back and summary.
  • Close: one takeaway from each woman, and what's next week.

Participant · Denise

Talks a lot

Your challenge:

  • You answer every question first and tell long stories.
  • You mean well. You want to help the younger women.
  • Stop if a co-facilitator redirects you kindly. If they're harsh, get quiet and hurt.

Participant · Ama

Quiet, English is her second language

Your challenge:

  • You understand most English but are afraid to speak in a group.
  • You have a gap because your employer took your passport and made you work without pay for two years.
  • You'll speak in a pair, but not to the whole group, unless invited gently.

Participant · Rochelle

Discloses

Your challenge:

  • Halfway through, you say, “My gap is because he put me in the hospital. Twice.” Your voice shakes.
  • If the facilitators handle it with care, you're okay and stay.
  • If they ask for details or ignore it, you leave the room.

Observer and timekeeper

Gives feedback each round

Your job:

  • Keep time: 15 minutes per round, then 5 minutes of feedback.
  • Tally: open questions, reflections, inviting quiet voices, redirecting, handling disclosure.
  • Give one thing that worked and one thing to try.

Rotate: after each round, two new people become co-facilitators.
