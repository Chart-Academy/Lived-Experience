Certified Survivor Workforce Facilitator · Module 05 · In-class packet

Team roles: three scenarios at Bright Start Training

For instructors: Print one packet per team. Cut the cards apart. Go over the ground rules before starting: no physical contact, and anyone can call “pause.” Rotate the facilitator role each scenario. Each learner needs a debrief form for each scenario they facilitate.
Remember: Brianna, the training site, and every person in the scenarios are fictional. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case Meet Brianna

Brianna, 24, is in week three of a culinary job training program for women. She is one of the most talented students. She left a violent relationship four months ago and lives with her aunt. She has a 2-year-old son. This week, her son was sick, she's slept very little, and her ex has been texting her. Today, three things happen at the training site.

“Everybody is always telling me to calm down. Nobody asks why I'm upset.”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 20 min on your own

Brianna

Training participant

What you're feeling:

  • Scenario 1: the chef says loudly, “Are you even trying?” You feel humiliated. You slam a pan down.
  • Scenario 2: your ex texts, “I know where your aunt lives.” You stop working and start pacing and crying.
  • Scenario 3: you're told you'll lose this week's $150 stipend for being late twice. You yell, “I was at the clinic with my son!”

How to play it: Calm down when the facilitator gives you space, names your feeling, and offers a choice. Get louder if they argue or crowd you.

Facilitator (rotates)

Program staff, de-escalates

Your goals each scenario (5 minutes):

  • Notice the warning signs and check safety.
  • Lower your voice, give space, name the feeling.
  • Offer a choice. Find out what she needs.
  • Know when to get help: Scenario 2 needs a safety response.

Chef instructor

Training kitchen

Only you know:

  • Scenario 1: you're under pressure. A catering order is due in two hours.
  • You think Brianna is one of your best students, but you never tell her.
  • After she calms down, you can apologize for calling her out in front of the class, if the facilitator helps.

Classmate

Another participant

Only you know:

  • You're friends with Brianna.
  • In Scenario 2, you know her ex has hurt her before. You want to help but don't know how.
  • In Scenario 3, you start to join in, yelling that the stipend rule is unfair.

You need from another role: Calm down if the facilitator speaks to you calmly and gives you a job, like getting water.

Program manager

Sets the stipend rules

Only you know:

  • Scenario 3: the rule says two late arrivals means losing the week's stipend.
  • You do have the power to excuse lateness for medical appointments with a note.
  • You'll make an exception if the facilitator explains Brianna's situation calmly.

Team lead and observer

Keeps time and runs the debrief

Your job:

  • Keep time: 5 minutes per scenario, then 10 minutes for the debrief form.
  • Call “pause” if anyone needs a break or a role-play gets too real.
  • Lead the team through the debrief form after each scenario.

Only you know: the program has a safety plan for situations like Scenario 2, including a private room, the security desk, and a phone to call the shelter's advocate.
