Certified Community Practitioner · Module 06 · In-class packet

Team roles: Kevin's first two weeks

For instructors: Print one packet per team. Cut the role cards apart and give one to each person. The listings on the cards are made up and out of date on purpose. Learners verify them against official websites, and by phone if possible. Make sure the room has internet, and set up a shared class resource guide before class.
Remember: Kevin is fictional. Never use real client names or details in class.

The case Meet Kevin

Kevin Brooks, 26, came home from prison two weeks ago. He is sleeping on a friend's couch, but his friend's lease doesn't allow it, so he has about two weeks. His state ID expired while he was inside, and he doesn't have his birth certificate. He has no income, and his phone only works on Wi-Fi. He wants to work, and he wants to see his 4-year-old daughter. Your agency's resource guide is old. Your team's job is to check each listing, fix what's wrong, and plan Kevin's first two weeks.

“I've got a list of 20 places people told me to go. I don't have bus fare for 20 places. Tell me which three.”

Step 1 Role cards · cut apart · 20 min on your own

Housing navigator

Shelter and housing

The old guide says:

  • “Shelter: call the main DHS number and ask for the shelter list.”
  • “Walk in to any shelter after 7 p.m.”

Verify: Find the current way to request shelter in DC, the hours, and whether there's a hotline. Note what Kevin should do if his couch falls through.

Benefits navigator

Food and benefits

The old guide says:

  • “SNAP: apply in person only, at the service center on your side of town.”
  • “Bring photo ID or you can't apply.”

Verify: Find the current ways to apply for SNAP and Medicaid in DC (online, phone, in person), and what to do if Kevin doesn't have ID yet.

Reentry navigator

Returning citizen services

The old guide says:

  • “Returning citizens office: open Tuesdays and Thursdays only.”
  • “Ask for the ID voucher at the front desk.”

Verify: Find MORCA's current location, hours, and services, and whether it helps with ID or birth certificate fees.

ID and documents navigator

ID and vital records

The old guide says:

  • “DC ID: bring any one document.”
  • “Birth certificates: same-day at the vital records office, cash only.”

Verify: Find the documents DC DMV requires for an ID card, and how to request a birth certificate, the cost, and how long it takes. Note: Kevin was born in DC.

Employment navigator

Jobs and training

The old guide says:

  • “Job center: walk in any time, ask for a counselor.”
  • “Ask about the transitional jobs program for people with records.”

Verify: Find the nearest American Job Center, its hours, and DOES programs for returning citizens, including what Kevin needs to enroll.

Team lead

Plans the order and the handoff

Your job:

  • Keep time and make sure every role reports back.
  • Start the team's verification log while others check.
  • Lead the team in choosing Kevin's first three stops, in order.

Only you know: Kevin's supervision officer requires him to look for work, and Kevin can get to most places only by walking or by bus fare from his friend.
