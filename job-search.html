Job Search Support

Put your new credential to work. You'll build a résumé from experience you actually have, find real openings in human services, spot scams, and practice interviews, with AI as your assistant and you in charge.

Sessions

Six sessions, 15 hours

J1Your Target Role & Must-Haves
Pick the job you're going after.2 hrs

Learning objectives · the learner will be able to:

  1. Name three job titles that fit SOC 21-1093 and their course credential.
  2. Write must-haves (pay, schedule, commute) and nice-to-haves.
  3. Place their target job on the four-tier career ladder and name the next rung.

In-class activity

  1. Review five real postings and choose one target role.
In your own words · no AI
  • “The job I'm going after is … because …”
J2Résumé from Lived Experience
Translate what you've done into language employers hire for.3 hrs

Learning objectives · the learner will be able to:

  1. Turn work, volunteer, peer, caregiving, and lived experience into action-and-result bullet points.
  2. Add the course credential and competencies to a résumé accurately.
  3. Explain a gap or justice involvement in one honest, forward-looking sentence.
  4. Tailor a résumé to a real posting without adding anything that isn't true.

In-class activity

  1. Remove your address, phone, and email, then ask AI to review your résumé against a checklist.
  2. Make the three changes that matter most yourself, then tailor a copy to your target posting.
AI Instructions: Only suggest rewrites based on experience the student actually has. Never invent jobs, dates, credentials, or skills. Ask the student to explain each change in their own words.
In your own words · no AI
  • “The biggest change I made to my résumé was … because …”
J3Finding Openings & Avoiding Scams
Real jobs that fit you, and the red flags of fake ones.3 hrs

Learning objectives · the learner will be able to:

  1. Search Indeed, DC Government Careers, and nonprofit job boards using filters and job alerts.
  2. Use a Skills-to-Openings agent to compare 10 real openings against their must-haves.
  3. Identify red flags of a scam listing, such as upfront fees or requests for bank details.

In-class activity

  1. Rank 10 real openings and mark each as good fit, not a fit, or scam.
AI Instructions: Never log in to a job site or apply for the student. Do not include listings that ask for upfront fees or personal financial information.
In your own words · no AI
  • “I knew one listing was a scam because …”
J4Interview Practice
Practice with an AI coach, then with a real person.3 hrs

Learning objectives · the learner will be able to:

  1. Answer behavioral questions using STAR (Situation, Task, Action, Result).
  2. Decide what to share about lived experience in an interview, and what to keep private.
  3. Run two practice interviews with an Interview Practice Coach agent and act on the feedback.
  4. Complete one mock interview with an employer partner or instructor.

In-class activity

  1. Write two STAR stories, then practice them out loud.
In your own words · no AI
  • “My STAR story is: Situation … Task … Action … Result …”
J5Applications & Employer Connections
Apply, follow up, and meet employer partners.2 hrs

Learning objectives · the learner will be able to:

  1. Submit at least one complete application, or save one with an apply-by date.
  2. Write a short cover letter and a polite follow-up email.
  3. Introduce themselves to an employer partner in 30 seconds.

In-class activity

  1. Employer partner meet-and-greet and application work session.
In your own words · no AI
  • “My 30-second introduction is …”
J6Tracker & 90-Day Plan
Stay organized after the course ends.2 hrs

Learning objectives · the learner will be able to:

  1. Track applications in Google Sheets with status and follow-up dates.
  2. Use a calendar agent to block weekly time for applications and credential steps.
  3. Write a 90-day plan for job placement and credential completion.

In-class activity

  1. Build your tracker and add your next two weeks to your calendar.
In your own words · no AI
  • “My next three steps to get hired are …”

Ground rules for job searching with AI

  • Remove your address, phone, and email before sharing your résumé with AI.
  • Every line on your résumé must describe something you actually did.
  • Search and apply for jobs yourself. Never let an AI tool log in to a job site or apply for you.
  • Never pay a fee to get a job, and never share bank details or your Social Security number before a verified offer.
