Getting Started

Day One Readiness Check

A short check of what you already know. It shows where you're starting so the course can help you grow. It's fine not to know the answers yet. About 25 minutes.

  1. Sign in with the Gmail address you gave your instructor.
  2. Enter your Student ID, not your name.
  3. Answer on your own.

Ground rules

Our community

  • You never have to share your own story to participate.
  • What's shared in the room stays in the room.
  • Take a break whenever you need one.
  • Speak from your own experience and respect others'.

Using AI in Job Search

  • Never enter real client information, or your own Social Security number, bank details, passwords, or medical records.
  • Check any fact that matters. AI can sound confident and still be wrong.
  • Say when you used AI, and cite your sources.
  • Write every “In your own words” section yourself.
